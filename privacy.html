Privacy Policy

Hisab — your calm khata book · Last updated 28 September 2026

Hisab is a personal ledger (khata) app for tracking purchases, dues, and money given or taken. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your records stay yours — we run no servers and receive none of your data.

Where your records are stored

  • Local accounts (username + password): records are stored only in your own browser's local storage on your device. They never leave your device.
  • Google accounts (Sign in with Google): records are stored in a hidden app folder (appDataFolder) inside your own Google Drive, plus a local copy in your browser so the app works offline. Only Hisab can read that hidden folder; it does not appear in your visible Drive files.

Google sign-in

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your basic profile with the app — your name, email address, and profile picture. This is used only to identify your account and keep each person's records separate. The app requests the Google Drive appData scope solely to save and load your own khata records as described above. It does not read, write, or access any other files in your Drive.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No data is sent to us or to any third party — there is no Hisab server.
  • Your records are never sold, shared, or used for any purpose besides showing them back to you.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: More → Clear this portal's entries deletes a portal's records, or use Export backup first if you want a copy.
  • Google accounts: you can also remove Hisab's access any time via your Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
  • Local accounts: clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on that device.

Security note

Logins in Hisab are convenience locks for family use, not bank-grade security. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser or Google account could view your records. Keep your devices and Google account secure.

Children

Hisab is a general household ledger and is not directed at children. Records are stored per account as described above regardless of the user's age.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please contact the person who shared the app with you (the site owner).
